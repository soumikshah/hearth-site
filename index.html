A clear guide for your family

Help your family understand which accounts, documents, and belongings you have, where to find them, and who can help.

What it is

A guide to your important information: what you have, where the originals are kept, who to contact, and what needs attention first.

Add a document or photo to an entry when it helps, such as an insurance policy or a property deed. Include where the original is kept, too.

What it isn’t

  • Not Drive or DropboxYour records stay on your device. Hearth doesn’t upload them to an online account.
  • Not a password managerRecord where passwords or recovery phrases are safely kept. Hearth is not designed to store those secrets.
  • Not a willHearth helps your family find your will and other legal documents. It does not replace them or provide legal advice.
  • Share funeral wishes separatelyTell someone you trust about your funeral wishes. Your family may need them before they can unlock your Hearth records.

How it works

  1. Add a little at a time

    Start with your will, a bank account, an insurance policy, and important contacts. Add more whenever you’re ready. Your entries and attachments are encrypted on your device.

  2. Choose your check-in schedule

    Check in with Hearth every two weeks, four weeks, or three months. You choose the schedule. While you keep checking in, access for the person you named stays locked.

  3. Leave a way to unlock your records

    Print your recovery sheet, keep it with your will, and tell the person you named where to find it. If you stop checking in for the time you chose, they can use the sheet to unlock your records.

What you can record

Group information by when your family is likely to need it, so they know where to start.

The first days
Where the will is and who drafted it. Who to call: lawyer, accountant, employer. Keys and who holds spares. Pets and dependents who need care right away.
The first weeks
Bank accounts, life and health insurance, pensions and provident funds, and identity documents. Where to claim, who to ask, and what the insurer will want to see.
Property and valuables
Property, vehicles, investments, lockers, jewelry, and where a crypto wallet’s keys are kept. Money that others owe you.
Bills and online accounts
Loans, credit cards, utilities, and subscriptions. Email, phone, and social accounts, plus what you’d like done with them.
More than one country?
Choose a country for each record. Your family can then view a separate checklist for each country. Records for the US and India use local terms. Other countries use general labels, such as “ID number” and “account number.”

Questions

Is this like Google Drive?

Hearth is a guide to your important information: which accounts and documents you have, where to find them, and who to contact. You can attach files to your entries, but everything stays on your device rather than in online storage.

What if my family can’t unlock it?

The person you name needs your printed recovery sheet to unlock your records once the check-in period has passed. Keep it with your will and tell them where to find it. We can’t reset access if they lose the sheet.

How often do I have to check in?

Choose every two weeks, four weeks, or three months. Pick a schedule that fits your routine. Hearth works offline, so you don’t need an internet connection to check in.

Does it replace a will or a lawyer?

No. Hearth helps your family find your will, insurance policies, and property documents. It does not replace legal documents or advice from a lawyer.

Which countries does it support?

You can organize records for any country. Records for the US and India use local terms. Other countries use general labels, and you can add notes for any extra details.

Where can I get Hearth?

You can get Hearth through Google Play. Check the store listing for device compatibility.

What does it cost?

Hearth is free for a limited time on Google Play. After that, you pay once. There’s no subscription and no account.
